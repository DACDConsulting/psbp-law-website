Immigration

Dallas Immigration Attorneys

Guidance on EB-5 investor visas, employment-based visas, family immigration, and pathways to permanent residency and citizenship.

Whether you are an individual seeking an investor visa, a company bringing talent from overseas, or a family hoping to reunite, the U.S. immigration system can be complex. PSBP Law helps clients navigate the process and secure the documents they need.

Our team assists with family-based immigration, employment-based visas, and investor options. We work closely with each client to evaluate needs and prepare filings carefully.

Key Immigration Services

EB-5 Investor Visas

Assistance with the Immigrant Investor Program, including direct and regional center investments that create jobs and lead to permanent residency.

Employment-Based Visas

H-1B, L-1, E-2, TN, and other temporary and permanent options for companies and professionals.

Family Immigration

Spouse, fiancé, and other family-based petitions, removal of conditions, and related matters.

PERM, I-140 & Adjustment

Labor certification, immigrant petitions, and adjustment of status or consular processing.
