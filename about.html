About the Firm

PSBP Law

A boutique law firm delivering efficient, results-driven counsel to local, regional, and international clients from our offices in Irving and Frisco, Texas.

Experience. Relationships. Results.

PSBP Law (Patel, Soni, Barsness & Prescott) is a Dallas-based boutique firm. We partner with individuals, startups, and established companies to achieve their objectives in Immigration, Real Estate, Corporate law, Securities, and Estate Planning.

From our Irving headquarters and Frisco office (by appointment), we leverage broad experience and extensive relationships to deliver practical results for clients nationwide. We take pride in clear communication, attention to detail, and treating every client as a partner.
